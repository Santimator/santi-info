Welcome to my site!

Feel invited to explore it at your own rhythm. You’ll find different facets of my work and interests—each one something I can bring to various kinds of projects, depending on what you need.

I am officially an intellectually gifted person—which in practice means my brain works as a kind of holisticizer: always making connections, thinking in abstractions, finding patterns across domains. This is particularly well suited for a series of activities and explains a lot about my career, my life… and this website :D

The process of becoming me started when I left my hometown Madrid for Lyon, France, to study bioinformatics and modelling at INSA. In there I dealt simultaneously with a) becoming an engineer in a foreign language while learning it, b) being enriched (I definitely call it that way nowadays) by my first cultural shock, and c) navigating the emotional challenges of youth and of staying far from my previous relational safety net.

From there, the path took me through Germany (cellular modeling research), back to France (software testing), to Mayotte in Africa (teaching music), and eventually to Madrid, where I’m based at the moment. Along the way I’ve worked in bioinformatics, project management, data analysis, credit control. Need special mention the one month walk alone in the Saint James Way crossing France by foot for the impact it had in my relationship with myself.

But being trained as an engineer didn’t put me off alternative theories and therapies—quite the opposite. I applied the same reason and critical thinking to approach them with a reasonable open mind, separating wheat from chaff, and found many things that make a lot of sense: If you go beyond the public prejudices there is way more to it to astrology, conspiracies, paranormal, mediumnity, aliens, alchemy, spirituality... disregarding them before hand is like not using AI because your grandma says it's the Devil. Similarly, I could see the contradictions in scientific organizations too. At the end of the day, we’re all humans doing human things and setting what you think you know aside and giving a fresh look at thinks, new an old, can do wonders for you.

What you'll find here reflects that journey: <a href="/engineering/" class="smj-link smj-link--engineering">engineering work grounded in rigorous training</a>, <a href="/teaching/" class="smj-link smj-link--teaching">teaching informed by real classroom and performance experience</a>, <a href="/personal-development/" class="smj-link smj-link--personal-dev">personal development practices I've studied formally (family constellations, taichi, qigong)</a>, and <a href="/the-artist/" class="smj-link smj-link--artist">creative expression that's been woven throughout—clarinet performance, dance and choreography, acting, and content creation via YouTube and podcasting</a>. I don’t separate these domains—they team up with each other in unexpected ways.

I work in Spanish, English, and French. Currently based in Madrid, though I am willing to work remotely and am open to projects anywhere. If you’re dealing with something that sits between categories, or needs someone who can see the technical and human dimensions simultaneously, we should talk.

Take your time exploring. Each section goes deeper into a specific area.
