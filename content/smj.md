Welcome to my site!

Feel invited to explore it at your own rhythm. You'll find different facets of my work and interests—each one something I can bring to various kinds of projects, depending on what you need.

I am officially an intellectually gifted person—which in practice means my brain works as a kind of *holisticizer*: always making connections, thinking in abstractions, finding patterns across domains. This explains a lot about my career, my life... and this website :D

The process of becoming who I am started taking shape when I left for Lyon, France, to study bioinformatics and modelling at INSA. It was a particular kind of triple initiation: mastering engineering in a language not yet fully mine, absorbing the shock of real cultural immersion, and navigating the emotional weight of youth and distance from everything familiar. That crucible built a resilience and curiosity that has stayed with me ever since.

From there, the path took me through Germany (cellular modeling research), back to France (software testing), to Mayotte in Africa (teaching music), and eventually to Madrid, where I've been based since 2018. Along the way I've worked in bioinformatics, project management, data analysis, credit control—each role adding another layer of understanding about how technical systems and human systems intersect.

Being trained as an engineer didn't put me off alternative theories and therapies—quite the opposite. I applied the same reason and critical thinking to approach them with a firm foot, separating wheat from chaff, and found many things that make a lot of sense. The same way I could see the contradictions in scientific organizations—at the end of the day, we're all humans doing human things.

What you'll find here reflects that journey: <a href="/engineering/" class="smj-link smj-link--engineering">engineering work grounded in rigorous training</a>, <a href="/teaching/" class="smj-link smj-link--teaching">teaching informed by real classroom and performance experience</a>, <a href="/personal-development/" class="smj-link smj-link--personal-dev">personal development practices I've studied formally (family constellations, taichi, qigong)</a>, and <a href="/the-artist/" class="smj-link smj-link--artist">creative expression that's been woven throughout—clarinet performance, dance and choreography, acting, and content creation via YouTube and podcasting</a>. I don't separate these domains—they inform each other in unexpected ways.

I work in Spanish, English, and French. Currently based in Madrid, though I work remotely and am open to projects anywhere. If you're dealing with something that sits between categories, or needs someone who can see the technical and human dimensions simultaneously, we should talk.

Take your time exploring. Each section goes deeper into a specific area. Reach out through whichever channel works for you—email is easiest, but I'm flexible.
